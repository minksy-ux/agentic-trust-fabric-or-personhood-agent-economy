# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Agentic Trust Fabric a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully
- Focusing on the technical and social goals of the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insulting remarks
- Disruptive behavior that impedes productive work

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may take appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions when representing the project.
